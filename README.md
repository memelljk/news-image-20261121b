One deterministic image that sums up the whole of Saturday 21 November 2026 —
the full UTC day, morning to night — drawn entirely by `main.py`
(Python 3, standard library only, no network, fixed data, byte-identical
across runs).

## What is in the picture

- **50 stories** across **7 regions**, each with its named sources:
  Europe 10 · Asia 9 · Americas 8 · Middle East + Africa 6 ·
  Oceania + Global 5 · Money + Markets 6 · Sport 6
- **6 consensus hero panels** — the stories that led every roundup that day:
  1. **400+** — Russia's 400-drone / 110-missile barrage on Ukraine, with
     2 Kh-101 missiles and a jet drone down in south-east Poland
  2. **-3.7%** — the AI sell-off pushes Wall Street into correction
     (S&P 500 at 6,910; Nasdaq -5.28% erases 2026's gains)
  3. **M6.8** — the deep Junin, Peru quake (129 km), felt hard in Lima
  4. **9-6** — the ICJ holds Myanmar responsible for genocide against the
     Rohingya; the judgment was read on 19 Nov and Myanmar rejected it on
     21 Nov, and the poster labels it exactly that way
  5. **100K** — the biggest post-Brexit UK–EU reset: 100,000 two-way
     youth visas, food checks scrapped, carbon markets linked
  6. **25+** — coordinated Gen Z protests across 25+ European countries
- **13 panels in total**: the 6 heroes plus 7 drawn scenes — Cyclone Akvan's
  landfall, Riyadh's third night of missile interceptions, the Nun River
  boat rescue, Voyager 1 a full light-day away, GTA VI's $3.79bn first-day
  record, the foiled Copenhagen concert plot, and the tanker ablaze in the
  Strait of Hormuz.
- **Full provenance**: a source tag on every story, a provenance strip at
  the foot, and date rails down both edges. Every story was cross-checked
  against at least two named outlets (AP, Reuters, BBC, Al Jazeera, FT,
  Guardian, France 24, OneTurn and wires).

## Figures as last verified on the day

- Kryvyi Rih double-tap strike: **2 dead, 60 wounded** (AP) — the final
  regional-administration count, not the early 42.
- Nigeria funeral boat, Nun River: police figures **4 dead, 24 rescued**
  (Reuters); the poster's list notes the locals' 300+ aboard claim.
- Las Vegas F1: **Verstappen starts on pole** — Antonelli was fastest in
  qualifying but a 5-place grid penalty drops him to P6; Russell starts P2
  (Formula1.com / FIA).
- ATP Finals: framed as a semi-final night **still in play** — no finalist
  is claimed, because none was decided when the day closed.
- COP31 Antalya: framed as a **deadlock** — closing plenary suspended and
  the fossil-fuel mention cut from the draft; no final deal is claimed.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py                          # writes ./entry.png
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py   # grader mode: writes only there
```

Output: `entry.png`, 1024 × 1024 PNG, well under 1 MiB, identical bytes on
every run.
